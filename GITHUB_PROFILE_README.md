## Research focus

My work combines **mechanistic simulation** with **machine learning** so models answer both:

> **What will the material do?**  
> **Why does it do it?**

Current directions include:

- **Nanomaterial electron transport** — voltage-dependent graph activation, Kirchhoff transport, percolation, network topology, internal edge-current dynamics, and GNN surrogates.
- **Computational catalysis for PFAS remediation** — adsorption modeling, machine-learned interatomic potentials, DFT/VASP validation, and multi-fidelity surface screening.
- **Interpretable materials AI** — physics-informed feature engineering, graph learning, high-throughput screening, uncertainty-aware selection, and structure–property modeling.
- **Scientific computing at scale** — Python, GPU learning, HPC/SLURM workflows, numerical solvers, and reproducible publication pipelines.

## Featured research repositories

### [Graph-Based Nanoparticle Necklace Network](https://github.com/oissakah/graph-based-nanoparticle-necklace-network)

Physics-based graph modeling of emergent non-Ohmic electron transport in self-assembled nanoparticle networks.

**Methods:** graph theory · Kirchhoff solvers · percolation · spectral analysis · network diagnostics · HPC  
**Public output:** [arXiv:2607.03698](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.03698)  
**Project story:** [oissakah.github.io/nanonecklace.html](https://oissakah.github.io/nanonecklace.html)

### [ML for Sustainable PKS/CaCO₃/HDPE Composites](https://github.com/oissakah/ML_PKS_HDPE)

Machine-learning framework for full tensile stress–strain prediction using experimentally grounded and physics-informed features.

**Published results:** Extra Trees `R² = 0.9998`; modulus-free model `MAPE = 0.46%`  
**Paper:** [Results in Materials, 2026, 100950](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rinma.2026.100950)  
**Project story:** [oissakah.github.io/ml-composites.html](https://oissakah.github.io/ml-composites.html)

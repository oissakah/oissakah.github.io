Flagship · Graph physics · GNN

Graph-based nanonecklace electron transport

Connect microscopic activation, percolation, and current redistribution to macroscopic I–V response.

Physics-based graph representation, Kirchhoff solver, internal edge-current diagnostics, connectivity analysis, and graph neural-network surrogate modeling.

Outcome: a mechanistic framework that exposes the evolving internal state rather than only fitting the terminal current.

DFT · MLIP · Environmental catalysis

PFAS adsorption and catalyst screening

Accelerate exploration of PFAS–surface interactions without giving up first-principles validation.

Atomistic structure generation, machine-learned interatomic potentials, DFT/VASP calculations, adsorption energetics, surface-environment comparisons, and descriptor analysis.

Outcome: a multi-fidelity computational workflow for identifying structure–energetics trends relevant to PFAS remediation.

Published · ML · Sustainable composites

Machine learning for HDPE/PKS/CaCO₃ composite design

Can experimentally grounded, physics-informed features predict the full tensile stress–strain response?

Eight experimental compositions, 8,284 stress–strain data points, ten benchmarked ML algorithms, physics-informed feature engineering, and SHAP interpretation.

Outcome: Extra Trees achieved R² = 0.9998; a modulus-free model retained 0.46% MAPE.

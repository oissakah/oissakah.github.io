Flagship · Graph physics · GNN

Graph-based nanonecklace electron transport

Connect microscopic activation, percolation, and current redistribution to macroscopic I–V response.

Physics-based graph representation, Kirchhoff solver, internal edge-current diagnostics, connectivity analysis, and graph neural-network surrogate modeling.

Outcome: a mechanistic framework that exposes the evolving internal state rather than only fitting the terminal current.
Project pageGitHubPreprint
DFT · MLIP · Environmental catalysis

PFAS adsorption and catalyst screening

Accelerate exploration of PFAS–surface interactions without giving up first-principles validation.

Atomistic structure generation, machine-learned interatomic potentials, DFT/VASP calculations, adsorption energetics, surface-facet comparisons, and descriptor analysis.

Outcome: a computational workflow for identifying structure–energetics trends relevant to PFAS remediation.
Machine learning · Sustainable composites

Tensile-property prediction for HDPE/PKS/CaCO₃ composites

Can experimental composition and processing variables be used to predict and tune tensile performance?

Feature engineering, classical ML, hyperparameter optimization, cross-validation, and comparison against experimental materials data.

Outcome: peer-reviewed 2026 work in Results in Materials.

CGCNN · Monte Carlo · Porous materials

High-throughput screening of MOF catalysts

Use learned crystal representations to reduce the search space for photoelectrochemical CO₂-reduction materials.

Crystal graph convolutional neural networks, Monte Carlo screening, structural descriptors, and interpretability analysis.

Outcome: an end-to-end materials-informatics workflow connecting crystal structure to screening decisions.
Battery materials · Materials informatics

Solid-electrolyte screening for sodium-ion batteries

Prioritize candidate solid electrolytes with favorable ion transport and stability before expensive simulation.

Machine learning, atomistic simulation, descriptor-based screening, and uncertainty-aware down-selection.

Battery modeling · Experiment

LiMn₂O₄ cathode modeling and synthesis

Connect porous-electrode behavior with experimentally synthesized cathode architectures.

Multiphase porous-electrode modeling of voltage behavior paired with solvothermal synthesis of porous LiMn₂O₄ microspheres.
